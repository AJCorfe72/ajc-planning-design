Adrian Corfe, Director

About Adrian

Adrian is an expert in the production of planning, building control and construction design information, and has worked in the field of architecture for over 30 years.

During this time, he has helped secure many planning permissions for a wide range of renewable, residential and commercial development clients.

He has strong skills in Vectorworks Landmark and is proficient in 2D/3D drawing using AutoCAD Map 2023, with expertise in creating 3D illustrative models in SketchUp and photo-realistic rendering packages including Twinmotion. Adrian also has strong desktop publishing abilities using Adobe Creative Suite, including InDesign, Photoshop and Illustrator. He is committed to using technical skills to deliver innovative design solutions and better project outcomes.

He has considerable experience in both new build design and the adaptation and conversion of existing buildings, from modern conversions through to historical and listed buildings. Throughout his career he has gained significant skills in using a range of applications and software packages to present designs and illustrative information to a high standard.
