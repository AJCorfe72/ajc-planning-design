Services

Architectural design services

From the first site layout to photo-realistic visuals, everything you need to present a proposal for planning.

01

Site layout design

Well-considered site layouts that balance the brief with access, boundaries, landscaping and planning policy.

  • Residential plots and housing layouts
  • Residential and holiday lodge parks, designed to the relevant model standards
  • Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) and infrastructure layouts
  • Feasibility layouts to test site capacity
02

Residential architectural design

Existing and proposed drawings for homes of all kinds, from new builds to sensitive conversions of historical and listed buildings.

  • Floor plans, elevations and sections
  • Extensions, alterations and conversions
  • Drawings for planning and building control
03

3D modelling & CGI illustrative visualisations

Once a site layout has been agreed and frozen, a 3D site model can be built and populated with indicative landscaping to give a realistic picture of the completed development.

  • 3D illustrative models in SketchUp
  • Photo-realistic renders in Twinmotion
  • Visuals for clients, planning officers and public consultation
04

Planning drawings

The essential plans that accompany any application, drawn accurately and to scale.

  • Site location plans for planning, permitting, title registration and licensing
  • Existing and proposed site block plans
  • Plans for feasibility studies
